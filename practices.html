Our Practice Areas

Comprehensive Legal Services Tailored to Your Needs

Areas of Legal Expertise

Our attorneys bring deep expertise across every major area of law, ensuring you receive the right counsel for your specific situation.

Corporate Law

We advise businesses of all sizes on corporate structure, mergers and acquisitions, shareholder agreements, regulatory compliance, and corporate governance. Whether you are launching a startup or managing a multinational, we are your trusted legal partner.

Civil Litigation

Our litigators are seasoned courtroom advocates with a track record of success in complex civil disputes. We handle breach of contract, tortious claims, commercial disputes and enforcement of judgments with strategic precision.

Real Estate Law

From purchase and sale agreements to title disputes and development financing, our real estate team provides comprehensive legal guidance for individuals, developers and institutional investors across all property transactions.

Family Law

We handle divorce, legal separation, child custody and support, adoption, prenuptial agreements and domestic matters with both compassion and legal rigour — protecting your family's best interests at every stage.

Criminal Defence

Our criminal defence attorneys provide aggressive, principled representation for clients facing charges at every level — from minor offences to serious felonies. We safeguard your rights and pursue every available defence.

Estate Planning

We craft wills, trusts, powers of attorney and comprehensive succession plans that protect your legacy and ensure your wishes are honoured. Our probate team also guides families through estate administration.

Employment Law

We represent both employers and employees in matters of wrongful termination, workplace discrimination, employment contracts, redundancy and labour disputes — ensuring fair and lawful outcomes for all parties.

Immigration Law

Our immigration attorneys assist individuals, families and corporations with visas, work permits, residency applications, citizenship and immigration appeals — navigating complex procedures with expertise.

Intellectual Property

We protect your innovations, brands and creative works through trademark registration, patent advisory, copyright enforcement and IP litigation — safeguarding what makes your business unique.
