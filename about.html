Our Story

Valerius, Thronus & Associates was founded in 1999 by senior partners with a shared vision — to build a law firm grounded in integrity, driven by excellence and bound by covenant to every client we serve.

Over 25 years, we have grown from a boutique practice into one of the region's most respected full-service law firms, handling matters that range from complex corporate transactions to deeply personal family disputes.

The griffin on our crest—a Historic symbol of Vigilance, Strenght, and Guardship—embodies our approach to the law. We stand watch over our clients' interests with fierce dedication, balancing power with the precise equity symbolized by the traditional scales of justice.

As Covenanted Counselors, we believe the relationship between an attorney and a client is a profound alliance. We do not offer assembly—line legal fixes; we provide deeply considered, strategic counsel tailored to complex legal landscapes. Our attorneys combine sharp academic precision with formidable courtroom advocacy, ensuring that your voice is heard and your interests are fiercely defended.

At Valerius, Thronus & Associates, we measure our success not just by the victories we achieve, but by the enduring trust we build with our clients. When you walk through our doors, you gain more than legal representation—you secure a formidable ally dedicated to upholding truth, balancing the scales, and delivering absolute justice.
